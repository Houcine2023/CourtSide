How it works

Three steps from looking to playing.

    Pick a club

    See the full day as a grid. Book straight away, or hold a slot for 10 minutes while your group confirms.

  3. 3

    Show up and play

    Cancelling frees the slot for someone else instantly, and notifies anyone waiting on the waitlist.
